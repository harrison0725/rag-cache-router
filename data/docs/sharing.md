# Sharing Files and Folders

You can share any file or folder by link or by inviting people by email.

Share links can be set to "Anyone with the link" or "Only people invited". On Plus and Business plans, links can also have a password and an expiration date. Free plan links never expire unless you delete them.

Shared folders: when you invite someone to a folder, they can be a Viewer, Commenter, or Editor. Files in a shared folder count only against the storage of the folder owner, not the people invited.

Business admins can restrict external sharing from the admin console under Sharing policies. The options are: allow sharing with anyone, allow only with approved domains, or block all external sharing.

File requests let people without a Lumen Drive account upload files to a folder you choose. File requests are available on all plans, but on the Free plan each request accepts a maximum of 100 MB in total.

Download limits: a single share link can be downloaded up to 200 GB per day on Plus and 1 TB per day on Business. On Free, a link is limited to 20 GB per day. If the limit is reached, the link is paused for 24 hours.
