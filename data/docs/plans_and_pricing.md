# Plans and Pricing

Lumen Drive offers three plans: Free, Plus, and Business.

The Free plan includes 5 GB of storage, file sharing by link, and version history for 7 days. It is limited to 3 devices per account.

The Plus plan costs $8 per month or $80 per year (two months free). It includes 1 TB of storage, version history for 90 days, unlimited devices, and password-protected share links.

The Business plan costs $15 per user per month with a minimum of 3 users. Each user gets 5 TB of storage. Business adds an admin console, single sign-on (SSO) with SAML, audit logs kept for 1 year, and version history for 365 days.

All paid plans can be billed monthly or annually. Annual billing is only available by credit card or invoice; PayPal can only be used for monthly billing.

Students with a valid .edu email address get 50% off the Plus plan for up to 4 years.

Prices are shown in US dollars. Local taxes such as VAT are added at checkout based on your billing address.
