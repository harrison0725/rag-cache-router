# Troubleshooting

Sync is stuck on "Indexing": this usually happens after adding a folder with many small files. Indexing about 100,000 files can take up to an hour. If it is stuck for more than 2 hours, quit the app and restart it. If it is still stuck, use Preferences > Advanced > Rebuild index.

Files are not uploading: check that you are not over your storage limit, that the file is under the size limit (50 GB in the desktop app), and that the file name does not contain characters that are not allowed: < > : " / \ | ? *. Names longer than 255 characters are also not supported.

"Too many devices" error: the Free plan allows 3 devices. Unlink an old device in Settings > Security > Devices, or upgrade to Plus for unlimited devices.

Slow uploads: check Preferences > Network to make sure a manual bandwidth limit is not set. Uploads over VPN can be slower; try pausing the VPN to test.

Cannot sign in: reset your password at lumendrive.example/reset. If your team uses SSO, you must sign in through your company identity provider instead of a password. If you lost your 2FA device, use a backup code.

Status page: current incidents and maintenance are posted at status.lumendrive.example. Support response times: Free users get email support with a reply within 3 business days, Plus users within 24 hours, and Business users get 24/7 chat and a 4-hour response time for urgent issues.
