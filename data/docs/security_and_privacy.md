# Security and Privacy

All files are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available on all plans. You can use an authenticator app, a hardware security key (FIDO2/WebAuthn), or SMS codes. Business admins can require 2FA for every user in the team.

End-to-end encrypted Vault: Plus and Business users get a Vault folder protected by a separate PIN. Files in the Vault are end-to-end encrypted, which means Lumen Drive staff cannot read them. Because of this, if you forget your Vault PIN and lose your recovery key, the files in the Vault cannot be recovered.

Data residency: by default data is stored in the United States. Business customers can choose to store data in the European Union (Frankfurt) or Australia (Sydney). Data residency must be chosen when the team is created and moving regions later requires contacting support and can take up to 14 days.

Lumen Drive is SOC 2 Type II certified and compliant with GDPR. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) can be signed by Business customers from the admin console.

Account recovery: if you lose access to your 2FA device, you can use one of the 10 backup codes generated when you set up 2FA. Without backup codes, identity verification by support takes 3 to 5 business days.
