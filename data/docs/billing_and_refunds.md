# Billing, Refunds, and Cancellation

You can cancel a paid plan at any time from Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. When you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, and then your account moves to the Free plan.

Refund policy: annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you keep access until the end of the month you paid for. To request a refund, contact support with your invoice number.

If you downgrade to Free and you are using more than 5 GB, your files are not deleted. Instead, your account becomes read-only: you can view, download, and delete files, but you cannot upload new files or sync until you are under the limit. Files over the limit are kept for 180 days, after which the oldest files over the limit may be deleted after two email warnings.

Failed payments: if a card payment fails, we retry three times over 10 days. After the final failed retry, the account is downgraded to Free.

Invoices can be downloaded from Settings > Billing > Invoices as PDF files. Business admins can add a VAT number and a billing contact email.

Upgrading from Plus to Business is prorated: you are charged only the difference for the rest of the current billing period.
