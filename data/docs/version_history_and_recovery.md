# Version History and File Recovery

Lumen Drive saves a new version every time a file changes. You can restore an older version from the file's menu > Version history.

How long versions are kept depends on your plan: 7 days on Free, 90 days on Plus, and 365 days on Business.

Deleted files go to the Trash. Files stay in the Trash for the same period as version history for your plan (7, 90, or 365 days), and then they are permanently deleted. Emptying the Trash manually deletes files immediately and they cannot be restored afterwards.

Rewind: Plus and Business users can rewind an entire folder (or the whole account) to a point in time, which is useful after a ransomware attack or a mass accidental deletion. Rewind is limited to the version history window of your plan.

Ransomware detection: on Plus and Business plans, Lumen Drive watches for many files being encrypted or renamed in a short time. If this happens, you get an email and an in-app alert with a one-click link to Rewind to the moment before the attack.

Files deleted by a team member in a Business shared folder can be restored by the folder owner or any Business admin.
